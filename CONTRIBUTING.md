# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. This project is offered under AGPL-3.0-only and an alternative commercial license, so TreeSeed must have permission to distribute accepted contributions under both models.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit it and grant the project owner a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, publicly perform, publicly display, sublicense, and relicense the contribution, including under the project's AGPL and commercial licenses. You retain ownership of your contribution.

Human-authored pull requests must include the checked human contributor affirmation in the repository pull-request template. A governed TreeSeed agent contribution instead requires a human-approved standing project authorization and an API-signed, assignment- and exact-head-bound agent attestation. Agents may populate that managed attestation only when both their definition and the repository `AGENTS.md` permit it; they must never check the human affirmation or create, broaden, renew, or revoke the standing authorization. The `Contributor License Grant` workflow verifies the selected mode and retains its evidence in the pull-request record. Contributions made on behalf of an employer require authority from that employer. A maintainer must not merge a contribution while its provenance or grant is unresolved.
